Storage accounts

discovered via on-chain access-control lists
💡 Why do I see storage accounts I never opened? An ACL entry only means your account has been granted a permission on that storage account — not that you created it. Token issuers and anchors (e.g. the USD/EUR stablecoins) create storage accounts to hold your token balances and grant your account access. DashKee checks the OWNER flag of each one, so you can see exactly which are yours.
